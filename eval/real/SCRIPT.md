# What to say

Record each line as its own voice memo, in your normal voice and at your normal speed, as if you just finished the job. Read the line as written (small slips are fine): the transcript is scored against these exact words.

Name each file `<your-first-name>__<id>.m4a`, for example `sam__c08-ambiguous-generator.m4a`.

| id | Say |
|---|---|
| `c01-clean-washer` | Finished the service on the pressure washer. Swapped two O-ring seals and one hose clamp, took about forty minutes. The pump seals were leaking. |
| `c02-clean-pump` | Water pump one, replaced two bearings, took an hour and a half. Bearing noise is gone. |
| `c04-clean-generator` | Tune-up on generator three. Replaced two spark plugs, thirty-five minutes. |
| `c08-ambiguous-generator` | Changed one air filter on the generator, thirty minutes. |
| `c10-ambiguous-filters` | Air compressor one, changed two filters, twenty-five minutes. |
| `c13-short-belts` | Generator one, replaced three drive belts, one hour. |
| `c15-missing-duration` | Shop vacuum one, replaced the air filter, just one. |
| `c17-correction-at-readback` | Laser level one, calibration check, no parts, thirty minutes. |
| `c20-multi-issue` | Generator service, used two drive belts and one filter, an hour. |
| `h02-ambiguous-mixer` | Concrete mixer, topped it up with two hydraulic fluid, fifteen minutes. |

That is all: the agent will ask follow-up questions during the eval, and the eval script answers those itself.
